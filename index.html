The author

Dr. Hafez Selim, MD, PhD

Hafez Selim is a physician-scientist who works across drug safety and clinical development at a global pharmaceutical company. Before that he spent nearly a decade as Medical Advisor and Safety Physician in the spine division of a leading medical device company, where he led safety for rhBMP-2 clinical trials and secured the first 510(k) clearance for PEEK rods.

His regulatory work spans the FDA, EMA, MHRA and PMDA. He has published 49 journal articles and holds four patents in collagen and biomaterial technologies.
